Privacy Policy

ArcanaDay Tarot & Horoscope  |  Last updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ArcanaDay Tarot & Horoscope ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Usage Data: Tarot readings, horoscope requests, and daily card selections you make within the app. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Language Preference: Your selected language (Turkish or English) to personalize your experience.
  • Device Information: Basic device information collected automatically by third-party services (see below).

We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information unless you choose to sign in with Google.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate personalized tarot and horoscope interpretations via AI (powered by OpenAI through our secure backend).
  • To save your reading history locally on your device.
  • To improve the app experience and fix issues.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob – for displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase (Google) – for secure backend processing of AI interpretations. No personal data is sent to Firebase beyond the content of your reading request. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI – AI interpretation requests are processed via OpenAI's API. Requests contain only the selected card names or zodiac sign — no personal information. OpenAI Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage

Your reading history and preferences are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. We do not store your personal data on our servers. You can delete all your data at any time from the app's Settings screen.

5. Children's Privacy

ArcanaDay Tarot & Horoscope is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Advertising

The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. These ads may be personalized based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and delete your locally stored reading history via the app's Settings.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.
  • Contact us with any privacy-related questions.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
